03 / THE MIC APPROACH

Understand first.
Confidence follows.

Learning gets easier when it has a structure. Use this simple loop to make every lecture more useful.

01

Understand the concept

Start with the “why” and “how”. Build the mental model before trying to memorise an answer.

◉
02

Revise with notes

Write down definitions, key points and confusing terms. Use available study material to revisit the topic.

▤
03

Practise and explain

Solve a few questions, speak the concept aloud and mark what still needs another look.
